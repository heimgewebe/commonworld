# Commonworld platform foundation v2

Status: implementation gate
Source: issue #181
Bound baseline: `19fa1d2206e58dd0b6d8ed95f28c78fd1319fc8e`

## Decision

Commonworld remains static, account-free and read-only. Broad catalogue expansion and public activation of candidate locales remain closed until operational truth, catalogue evidence debt and locale release evidence are machine-checked. Fully evidenced pilot entries remain possible when they do not increase basis debt.

## Ordered work

1. Terminalize the open locale closeout and reconcile active work without touching foreign dirty states.
2. Enforce the platform foundation gate in validation and CI.
3. Reduce the legacy Commons-basis queue to a reviewed reference corpus.
4. Compare additive CommonProject v4.1 with structural v5 using difficult archetype fixtures before migration.
5. Improve trust and discovery surfaces only after their source and derivation contracts are explicit.
6. Promote locales only after language, RTL, keyboard, screen-reader and state-preservation evidence is complete.
7. Expand in small evidence-bound waves.

## Foundation exit criteria

- catalogue entry, basis and debt counts are derived from canonical files;
- basis debt cannot increase silently;
- activity status sets cannot drift between current-state and project schema;
- candidate locales remain non-selectable and noindex;
- release storage is not redesigned without a measured rollback/churn comparison;
- a data-model migration is not authorized without a v4.1/v5 ADR.

## Non-goals

No mass import, locale release, backend, framework rewrite, vector search, automatic classification or release-tree relocation is part of this block.

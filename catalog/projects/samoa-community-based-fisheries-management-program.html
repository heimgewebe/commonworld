Samoa Community-Based Fisheries Management Program

← Commonworld catalog

A Samoa-wide commons infrastructure in which more than 120 coastal village communities develop and carry their own fisheries management plans, village bylaws and locally governed fish reserves with technical support from the national Fisheries Division.

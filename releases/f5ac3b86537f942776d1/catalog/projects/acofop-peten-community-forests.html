ACOFOP – Community Forests of Petén

← Commonworld catalog

A network run by peasant and Indigenous organisations in Petén connecting community forest concessions in the Maya Biosphere Reserve through democratic representation, territorial protection, fire control, restoration and sustainable forest use.

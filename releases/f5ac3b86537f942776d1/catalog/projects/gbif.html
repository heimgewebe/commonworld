Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)

← Commonworld catalog

A globally federated biodiversity data infrastructure in which participating countries, organizations, nodes and data publishers jointly provide data, standards and technical services for open biodiversity access and coordinate them through formal governance bodies.

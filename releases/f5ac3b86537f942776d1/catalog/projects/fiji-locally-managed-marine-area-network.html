Fiji Locally Managed Marine Area Network (FLMMA)

← Commonworld catalog

A Fiji-wide support network for locally managed marine areas in which communities use customary and collectively agreed rules, local monitoring and shared stewardship to manage inshore fisheries and marine habitats.

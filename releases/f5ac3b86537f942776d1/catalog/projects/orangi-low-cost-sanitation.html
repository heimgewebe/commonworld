Orangi Low-Cost Sanitation

← Commonworld catalog

A community-organized sanitation practice from Orangi in Karachi in which neighbourhoods jointly finance, build, manage and maintain household connections, lane sewers and secondary sewers, while OPP-RTI provides technical guidance and public authorities remain responsible for trunk infrastructure.

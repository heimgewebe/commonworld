National Union of Water Users’ Associations of the Kyrgyz Republic

← Commonworld catalog

A national union in Kyrgyzstan that supports community-based water users’ associations which organize water users, collectively maintain on-farm irrigation systems, coordinate joint action and represent members’ water-management interests.
